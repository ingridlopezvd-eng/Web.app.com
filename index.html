Community Resources in San Francisco

San Francisco is a wonderful vibrant and beautiful city, known for its iconic views, diverse culture, and creative energy. Yet even amid its beauty, many of our communities
continue to face challenges that can make day-to-day life difficult. It’s important to recognize these struggles and support one another by connecting with the many resources
available throughout the city. From community centers and food programs to housing support, mental-health services, and job-readiness programs, San Francisco offers a variety
of tool that we can take advantage of to help strengthen our neighborhoods and uplift those who need it most.
